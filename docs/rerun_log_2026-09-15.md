## Independent audit — 2026-09-15 (post-rerun)

### Stage 1 gate — passes, but has blind spots

- The 2Ω tone check cannot fail for almost any quadratic diagnostic of a
  symmetric orbiting pair — it follows from the pair's symmetry, not from a
  specific property of correct acoustics.
- Swapping in the wrong 3D Green's kernel (`e^{ikr}/(4πr)` instead of the 2D
  Hankel kernel) still passes the m=2 phase-winding check (measured
  m=-2.002 with the wrong kernel too). The magnitude-decay test would catch
  it, though: the 2D kernel gives an |p| ratio of 2.00 between r and 4r
  (correct 1/√r decay); the 3D kernel gives 4.00 (1/r decay). This check is
  not currently in the gate.
  **Status (follow-up):** Fixed: gate CHECK 3 added (|p| ratio r=40L vs
  160L must be 2.00).
- The code uses H₀⁽¹⁾ (an incoming-wave convention under numpy's FFT sign
  convention). Measured dφ/dr = +k at the tone frequency; an outgoing wave
  under this FFT convention needs dφ/dr = −k. Swapping to H₀⁽²⁾ flips the
  sign with no other change needed. This affects far-field phase (relevant
  to Stage 3), not the Stage 2 magnitude-based headline metric.
  **Status (follow-up):** Fixed: kernel now (−i/4)H₀⁽²⁾ in both exact and
  far-field branches; gate CHECK 4 verifies dφ/dr = −k.
- The Müller–Obermeier analytic amplitude comparison that the build plan
  specifies as the actual validation target was never implemented; the code
  documents this as deliberately deferred.
  **Status (follow-up):** Still open.
- `pytest` is not installed in this environment, so the README's "14 tests,
  all passing" claim could not be independently verified here.
  **Status (follow-up):** Resolved: pytest installed; full suite 21 passed
  (README's "14 tests" claim is stale).

### Cone-energy concentration (new caveat)

At every Mach number tested, essentially all of the radiating-cone energy
sits in a single wavenumber bin, |k|=1 — the largest scale the periodic box
allows (100% of cone energy at M=0.05, 100% at M=0.1, 96% at M=0.2, 90% at
M=0.3). At M=0.1 the acoustic wavelength (~8 length units) exceeds the box
size (L=2π≈6.28). The headline number is therefore effectively measuring the
high-frequency tail of the box-scale mode, not a general turbulence
property — it likely depends on domain size and forcing wavenumber (k=8) and
should be checked against a larger box or a different forcing scale before
being reported as a general scaling result.

**Status (follow-up):** Tested: see Step 6 in Follow-up session — confirmed
as a domain effect for R(all k).

### Non-power-law Mach scaling

Local exponents between the four Mach sweep points are 10.7 (0.05→0.1), 7.1
(0.1→0.2), and 5.7 (0.2→0.3) — not a single M⁶–M⁷ power law as the Stage 2b
summary above states. No theoretical scaling curve was computed to compare
against (build-plan Stage 2, step 10, remains undone).

**Status (follow-up):** Wording corrected in Stage 2b; a random-sweeping
reference model was checked and does not fit — see Follow-up session.

### Source spectrum directly (new measurement)

Computing R on the Lighthill source S itself (Hann, 4 segments), rather than
on velocity u, gives R = 2.56e-11 (M=0.05), 1.03e-08 (M=0.1), 7.49e-07
(M=0.2), 7.42e-06 (M=0.3) — about 8–16× smaller than the velocity-based R at
each Mach number. Since S is what actually radiates, this is arguably a more
direct number for the headline claim and is worth reporting alongside the
velocity-based R.

### B5. Noise-floor check (is the cone content at k=1 real or numerical?)

Artifacts: `runs/noise_floor_check/` (analysis.py, results.json,
baseline_komega_profiles.png, kf16_komega_profiles.png). Datasets: baseline
and forcing k=16.

- **float32 storage floor:** spectrum of uniform rounding noise (±0.5·np.spacing(float32)) sits ~1e-21; spectrum at k=1, ω = c0 (M=0.1) is ~3e-8 (≈13 orders above). Adding that noise changes R by ≤ ~1e-6 relative at all Mach.
- **Window leakage:** at thresholds, Blackman-Harris/Hann and DPSS/Hann power ratios (same segment length) are 0.72–1.15 everywhere except baseline M=0.05, k=2 (2.55).
- **Aliasing:** dt×2 subsampling changes the k=1 spectrum by ≤34% in ω∈[5,35] (baseline), ≤10% (k=16), concentrated near the reduced Nyquist edge.
- **Headline verdict:** at M=0.1 (k=1, ω≈11.3) all estimators agree and the spectrum is still decaying steeply — R measures real temporal content, not float precision.
- **Correction to the check agent's claim** that the production Hann-4seg spectrum "never floors": beyond ω≈35 its slowly decaying tail is window leakage (it sits well above the full-record Hann estimate; Blackman-Harris/DPSS plateau near 1e-13.7 there). A "still decaying" test cannot distinguish that from physics. It is ~6 orders below the level at ω≈11.3 and contributes <1% of R at M≥0.05.
- **Method note:** comparing per-bin power across estimators with different segment lengths is invalid — per-bin power scales with dω, so the full-record estimate (1024 samples) reads ~2.5× (0.40 decades) low. Compare densities (P/dω). Also, single-bin comparisons against a full-record periodogram (~2 dof/bin, ~100% scatter) are too noisy; integrated cone energy per estimator is the correct resolution test.
- **Unresolved cells:** baseline M=0.05 at k=2 and k=3 (thresholds ω≈45, 68). Forcing k=16: M=0.05, k=3 (ω≈47) marginal.

**Step 6 conclusions:**

1. **R(all k) is domain-scoped.** Doubling the box moves the cone energy
   into the new gravest mode and raises R 8.7× at M=0.1 (bands do not
   overlap), 42× at M=0.05, 2.0× at M=0.2, 1.3× at M=0.3. It is resolved,
   and not a float-precision artifact — it is a genuine property of the
   box-scale mode. It must not be quoted as a flow property at M≤0.1.
2. **Cone share over modes present in both boxes (k≥2) is approximately
   box-independent**: 1.3e-10 (4π) vs 3.8e-10 (2π) at M=0.1, inside the 2π
   run's band [1.1e-10, 3.8e-10] (envelope over full record and both halves;
   also inside the halves-only range [1.1e-10, 2.0e-10]); within 2.6× at M=0.2 and 1.8× at
   M=0.3. It depends on forcing scale (5.7e-09 with forcing k=16).
3. **The source-based share R_S is the most robust number measured:** at
   M=0.1, 7.3e-09 / 1.0e-08 / 7.0e-09 across L4π / baseline / kf16 (within
   1.5× across both box-size and forcing-scale changes); within ~2.3× at
   M=0.2–0.3 and ~3× at M=0.05. Caveat: in the 4π box ~77% of its cone
   energy sits at k=0.5 and its per-estimator resolution was not checked —
   the invariance is measured, not explained.
4. Across all three configurations and all measures, the cone share at
   M≤0.1 is ≤ ~1e-6 of the objective. The qualitative claim (L2 assigns
   negligible weight to the radiating cone) holds; the specific headline
   magnitude is domain-dependent for R(all k).
